================================================================================
               ODYSSEYAI - AUTONOMOUS AI TRAVEL PLANNER
                 PROJECT OVERVIEW, SPECS & ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

1. PROJECT SUMMARY (WHAT IS ODYSSEYAI?)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OdysseyAi is an intelligent, full-stack travel planning web application that 
transforms how people plan vacations. Instead of spending hours or days 
manually searching across travel blogs, booking sites, map calculators, and 
weather forecasts, a user simply enters their destination, dates, budget, and 
interests.

OdysseyAi automatically pulls real-world tourist attractions from Google Places, 
fetches live verified hotel prices from SerpApi Google Hotels, gathers live 
weather forecasts, calculates real road driving distances and fuel costs, and 
uses Google's Gemini LLM to create a day-by-day, hour-by-hour customized trip 
itinerary with built-in team collaboration.


2. PROJECT GOAL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The primary goal of OdysseyAi is to build an all-in-one, automated travel 
ecosystem that eliminates the stress, fragmentation, and inaccuracies of manual 
vacation planning.

Key Objectives:
- Zero-Hallucination Recommendations: Always ground AI suggestions in real-world 
  places, live hotel rates, and verified geographical coordinates.
- Accurate Budget Forecasting: Accurately estimate total trip costs, combining 
  hotel stays, daily activity entrance fees, and real-road vehicle fuel expenses.
- Collaborative Travel: Enable friends, families, and travel groups to plan 
  together using granular roles (Owner, Editor, Viewer).
- Proactive Risk Prediction: Warn travelers in advance about weather disruptions, 
  peak-season surge prices, long driving times, and monument closing days.


3. WHY IS IT NEEDED? (PROBLEMS SOLVED)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Current travel planning is fragmented, exhausting, and unreliable:

1. Information Overload & Tab Fatigue:
   - Problem: A traveler opens 15-25 browser tabs across Google Maps, TripAdvisor, 
     Booking.com, weather sites, and travel blogs.
   - Solution: OdysseyAi unifies places, hotels, weather, routes, and itineraries 
     into a single, clean dashboard.

2. Inaccurate Budget Surprises:
   - Problem: Most travel planners only estimate flight or hotel costs, ignoring 
     fuel costs, taxi fares, and local sight entry fees.
   - Solution: OdysseyAi calculates real road travel distance and fuel costs 
     (petrol/diesel) plus activity fees, showing a transparent Total Amount.

3. AI Hallucination & Fake Places:
   - Problem: Generic AI chatbots (like ChatGPT) frequently invent hotels, wrong 
     prices, or attractions that closed years ago.
   - Solution: OdysseyAi feeds REAL Google Places and live SerpApi hotel tokens 
     into the prompt. The AI is strictly instructed to never invent places or prices.

4. Group Travel Chaos:
   - Problem: Coordinating plans across WhatsApp chats leads to confusion over 
     who agreed to what, accidental schedule overwrites, and lost bookings.
   - Solution: Built-in 3-Tier team permission system (Owner, Editor, Viewer) 
     where everyone views the same live schedule with controlled editing rights.


4. KEY BENEFITS FOR TRAVELERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Save 90% of Planning Time: Generate a multi-day itinerary in under 30 seconds.
- Total Financial Transparency: Know exactly how much the trip will cost before 
  leaving home.
- Real-Time Live Hotel Rates: Real hotel photos, star classes, and room rates 
  powered by SerpApi.
- Intelligent Travel Personas: Customize your account with curated traveler 
  avatars (Cool Explorer, Curious Wanderer, Neon Voyager, Majestic Nomad, Sunset

5. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS (TECH STACK & ARCHITECTURE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Backend:
- Language & Runtime: Java 21 (LTS)
- Framework: Spring Boot 3.x
- Security: Spring Security 6 with stateless JWT (Access Token + Refresh Token), 
  BCrypt password encryption, and Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).
- Database: PostgreSQL with Spring Data JPA & Hibernate ORM.
- Database Migrations & Relations: Foreign keys with automated cascading cleanup 
  for Trips, Members, Itineraries, and Expenses.
- API Documentation: OpenAPI 3 / Swagger UI (`/v3/api-docs`).

Frontend:
- Runtime & Bundler: Node.js, Vite 8
- Language: TypeScript, React 19
- Styling: Tailwind CSS, custom modern responsive utilities, and interactive 
  pointer-reactive `BorderGlow` component.
- Icons & UI: Lucide-React, React Hot Toast.
- HTTP Client: Axios with automatic JWT bearer interceptors and token refresh.

External APIs & AI Integration:
- Google Places API (New): Sights, landmarks, restaurants within 25km radius.
- SerpApi (Google Hotels Engine & Local Photos): Live hotel rates, amenities, 
  and high-resolution photography.
- Google Routes API: Turn-by-turn driving meters, duration, and toll/fare data.
- Open-Meteo Weather API: Multi-day temperature min/max, precipitation, and rain 
  probability.
- Google Gemini LLM: Reasoning engine for curation, day-by-day scheduling, and 
  proactive risk prediction.


6. STEP-BY-STEP DATA FLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Step 1: User Input]
The user enters:
- Destination (e.g. "Goa, India")
- Dates (e.g. 2026-10-10 to 2026-10-15 = 5 Days)
- Total Budget (e.g. ₹40,000)
- Travelers count & Personal Interests (e.g. "beaches, seafood, historical forts")

[Step 2: External Travel Data Ingestion (Parallel Fetch)]
1. Google Places Client queries nearby coordinates for:
   - `tourist_attraction`
   - `historical_landmark`
   - `restaurant`
2. SerpApi Hotel Client queries Google Hotels for the destination and dates, 
   parsing up to 40 verified hotels with room prices and photos.
3. Open-Meteo Weather Client retrieves daily forecasts for the trip dates.
4. Fallback Image Enricher ensures every place and hotel has a high-res photo 
   even if external photo limits are reached.

[Step 3: AI Recommendation & Risk Analysis]
1. `RecommendationPromptBuilder` gathers up to 35 places, 25 hotels, weather 
   forecasts, and user budget.
2. The structured prompt is sent to Google Gemini LLM.
3. Gemini evaluates ratings, category diversity, and weather compatibility.
4. Gemini returns structured JSON containing:
   - Curated places (12-16 top recommendations)
   - Curated hotels (8-12 verified stays matching budget)
   - 3 to 5 `potentialProblems` (weather warnings, traffic, closing days)

[Step 4: Interactive Selection on Frontend]
1. User reviews the interactive cards wrapped in `BorderGlow`.
2. User can choose between:
   - Auto AI Plan: Let Gemini schedule the trip automatically.
   - Manual Plan: Pick exact places and hotels manually.
3. Live Dynamic Calculator updates Total Cost (Hotels + Activities) in real time.

[Step 5: Road Distance & Fuel Expense Calculation]
1. The backend resolves the geographical sequence of the chosen places.
2. `ItineraryRouteService` computes real road distance in kilometers using 
   Google Routes (with Haversine * 1.35 road tortuosity fallback).
3. Fuel costs are calculated using standard benchmark rates:
   - Petrol: ~₹14.00 per km
   - Diesel: ~₹11.50 per km

[Step 6: Final Itinerary Generation]
1. `PromptBuilder` provides the AI with up to 20 attractions, 15 restaurants, 
   and 6 route pairs.
2. Gemini generates an exact hour-by-hour daily schedule:
   - Day 1: Morning, Afternoon, Evening stops with descriptions and entry costs.
   - Day 2, Day 3, etc.
3. The itinerary is saved to PostgreSQL linked to the Trip entity.

[Step 7: Collaboration & Permission Control]
1. Trip Owner invites co-travelers via email with a role:
   - EDITOR: Can modify days, add activities, change trip title/dates.
   - VIEWER: Read-only access to view the itinerary and routes.
2. Only the Trip OWNER has full control to delete the trip or remove members.


7. PERMISSION & ROLE MODEL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
System-Wide Roles:
- ROOT ADMIN: Access to the Admin Console (`/admin`), can inspect all accounts, 
  change roles, lock/unlock accounts, and purge users.
- USER: Regular authenticated traveler.

Trip-Level Collaboration Roles:
- TRIP OWNER:
  - The creator of the trip.
  - Full control: edit trip, generate itinerary, invite/remove members, delete trip.
- EDITOR:
  - Invited collaborator.
  - Can modify itinerary activities, update trip details, add notes.
  - Cannot delete the trip or remove other members.
- VIEWER:
  - Invited guest/collaborator.
  - Can view trip itinerary, weather, and costs.
  - Cannot make edits or delete items.


8. PROJECT SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Current Scope (Implemented & Live):
- User authentication (Register, Login, Password Eye toggle, JWT security).
- User Profile management with 5 selectable Travel Persona Avatars.
- Admin Console for comprehensive user management and security monitoring.
- AI Trip Recommendations with SerpApi (40 hotels) and Google Places (35 places).
- Intelligent Risk & Problem Prediction (weather, traffic, closing warnings).
- Real road distance and vehicle fuel cost calculation (petrol/diesel).
- Day-by-day, hour-by-hour itinerary generator with activity deletion and cost tallies.
- 3-Tier Trip Collaboration (Owner, Editor, Viewer).
- Fully responsive modern UI with BorderGlow effects across mobile and desktop.

Future Scope (Planned Enhancements):

- Live Multi-Currency Conversion with dynamic exchange rate updates.
- Real-Time Collaborative Chat & Comments inside trip workspaces.
- Offline Mobile App (PWA with offline cached itineraries).
